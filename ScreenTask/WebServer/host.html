Share your screen directly with one viewer. Video and audio use a direct WebRTC connection. Browser capture is paired by ScreenTask; HDR mode also reads corrected monitor frames locally. No TURN relay is configured.

Use your LAN address for nearby viewers. For remote friends, enter your public address and external TCP port, or HTTPS address. The external port may differ from ScreenTask’s listening port. Test this link from the viewer’s network.

Enable HDR correction on the app’s Video tab first. This shares the entire monitor selected there, rather than a browser-picked window. Tone mapping happens before direct WebRTC transmission. Local decoding and re-encoding add some latency.

Optional: select the same monitor in the picker and enable system audio. Video still comes from the monitor selected in ScreenTask.

Waiting to start.

The host page must be opened on this PC at localhost. For a friend outside your home network, they also need a way to reach the viewer page for signaling (usually a router port forward or an HTTPS tunnel). This connection will still fail if a firewall/NAT blocks direct WebRTC.

Allow system audio in the browser's screen picker if you want audio. Browser support varies.
